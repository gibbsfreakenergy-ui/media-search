💡 Direct Streaming Guide:

To pull media onto your site without database tokens, simply input the title's unique **IMDb ID** directly into your player path.

  • Go to any public film database website and search your favorite title.
  • Copy the unique ID code directly out of your browser's address field (it always starts with a tt followed by matching numbers, like tt1375666).
  • Paste it directly into your dashboard stream input and hit click to watch instantly!
